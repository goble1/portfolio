## Coursework

- Watch course videos at 1.25× speed — 1 hour/day = ~3 months
- Make one flashcard per video — keep it focused and brief
- Use Anki on desktop to copy/paste content efficiently
- Spaced repetition (via Anki) boosts long-term recall — used by doctors and lawyers

## Flashcards

- Prioritize active recall
- Daily routine:
  1. Review existing flashcards (morning)
  2. Watch videos or take a practice exam and create new cards
  3. Review new cards before bed

### Anki Setup

- Set max review interval to 30 days
- Show review cards before new ones

### Card Types

- **Fact-based**:  
  Example: "How much temporary storage do Lambda functions have?"

- **Conceptual**:  
  Example: "How do SQS queues trigger Lambda functions?"

## Practice Tests

- Focus on reviewing, not just taking the test
- While taking tests, rate each question:
  - **1** – No idea
  - **2** – Educated guess
  - **3** – Partial understanding
  - **4** – Full confidence

- During review:
  - Skip questions rated 4 and answered correctly
  - Focus on 1–3s and any 4s you guessed

## Why Certifications?

Certifications make it easy to justify a score of 5 in your self-review growth category.

## Pro Tips

- Certifications are often reimbursable through your company 
- It adds credibility to your skills on linked in
