## Garage door opener

### Design

I wanted a way to open my garage door with my phone. While most apps are around $75 and require a subscription. I got a microcontroller with a built in server. I listens for garage.local and then then turns on voltage on two pins connecting the relay and completing the circut. This is the same way the door bell garage door open works by completeting the secret.
[link to code](github.com/goble1/gragedoor)

### Parts
- ELEGOO Nano V3.0 Board
- WRL-13764 Particle Photon
- AEDIKO 4pcs DC 12V Relay Module
- snack box
